DRQ HOME MONITOR — App Review Demonstration

This page hosts the screen recording requested by App Review for DRQ HOME MONITOR (bundle id com.drquezada.homemonitor). The recording was captured on a physical iPhone and begins with launching the app.

Direct download: drq-demo.mp4 (H.264, approx. 10 MB, 6 minutes)

The recording shows the following flow:

  1. Launching the app from the iPhone home screen.
  2. Setting up the anonymous profile (a patient code, year of birth, height and sex — no name or personal identifiers are ever requested).
  3. Recording measurements: blood pressure, capillary glucose with meal context, oxygen saturation, and weight/height with automatic BMI calculation.
  4. Adding a medication with dose, treatment days and daily times, including the iOS notification permission prompt used for local medication reminders.
  5. Reviewing saved records in the history tab.
  6. Generating the anonymized PDF report and opening it through the standard iOS share sheet.
All data shown in the recording is sample data entered for demonstration purposes. The app requires no account or sign-in, makes no network requests, and stores all records locally on the device.
